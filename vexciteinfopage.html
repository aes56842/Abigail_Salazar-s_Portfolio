The Electric Motorcycle

Vexcite, the electric motorcycle. In this project, I along with other peers were paired up with the co-founder of Vexcite Bikes to identify how we could get Vexcite out to their target audience. We conducted market research, comparing other electric bikes and general means of transport to identify how we can best stand out. In addition we conducted a variety of interviews targeting both our target audience and those who aren't to observe how we can best serve users and convert potential customers. Lastly, we helped re-design their website to emphasize their new improvements and influence visitors to join their waitlist. Please check out Vexcite's new page, https://www.vexcitemk2.com, to see our work.
