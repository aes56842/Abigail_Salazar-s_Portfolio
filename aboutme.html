About Me

Hello! Thank you for checking out my portfolio! I'm, Abigail Salazar, a designer working across branding, product design, and experience building. I'm drawn to projects that have a purposeful mission at heart and where a visual identity has to hold up across many surfaces — a logo mark, a package, a screen — and still feel like one consistent idea. My favorite work revolves helping communities, people and those in need, and has resulted from pro-bono work. Feel free to contact me if you'd like to work together, I'm always open to a new project!

Good design isn't decoration. It's the clearest version of an idea.

A little bit about myself, I'm currently a Junior at the University of Southern California (USC). Majoring in "Arts, Technology and Business of Innovation" under the Iovine and Young Academy. I was born and raised in the Southern Los Angeles area and love to spend time with my dog, visit the beach and love to draw!
