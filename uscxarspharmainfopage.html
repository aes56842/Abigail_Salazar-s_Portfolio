Anaphylaxis Prevention on Campus

Anaphylaxis, a severe life-threatining allergic reaction. In this project, we partnered with Dan Revolsky, Senior Vice President of Strategic Partnerships & Commercial Alliances at ARS Pharmaceuticals, Inc. and tasked to help college campuses become safer against anaphylactic emergencies by identifying and implementing preventative measures while spreading awareness of these emergencies and their common occurances.
